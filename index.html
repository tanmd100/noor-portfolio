I didn't come from a technology background. With over 25 years in business operations — from international procurement across UAE, Europe and Kazakhstan, to managing HR and sales across a 5-branch, 250+ employee automobile dealership — my career has been built on understanding how businesses actually work.

Project 01  ·  AI & LLM

JATIP — AI Candidate Screening Platform

An end-to-end LLM-powered hiring intelligence system that automates the most time-consuming part of recruitment: evaluating candidates fairly and consistently at scale, across multiple intake channels.

Project 02  ·  Customer Automation

WhatsApp Service Reminder Automation

A production-deployed automation currently active at JAPL — reads vehicle service-due data from Excel and sends personalised, bilingual WhatsApp reminders, eliminating hours of manual customer follow-up every week.

Sends personalised English + Kannada reminders at 7, 5, 3, and 1 day(s) before each customer's service due date
Includes mobile number validation, dynamic multilingual message generation, full error handling and WhatsApp Desktop automation
Logs all activity to CSV for full audit trail — deployed and running in a real, 5-branch business environment

Project 03  ·  SMS Automation

SMS Reminder Gateway

A companion automation to the WhatsApp system — generates personalised SMS reminder content and exports it for delivery via Android SMS gateway. Packaged as a standalone Windows .exe, currently in live use — no Python required on the end machine.

Generates personalised SMS on configurable date thresholds; exports to Google Drive-synced CSV for the Android SMS app
Handles execution logging, error handling, and bulk SMS delivery without a commercial SMS API subscription
Packaged as a portable Windows .exe using PyInstaller — fully self-contained, zero installation required on the target machine
